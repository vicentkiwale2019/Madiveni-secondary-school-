About Our School

Madiveni Secondary School

Madiveni Secondary School is located in Same, Mbakweni, Kilimanjaro, Tanzania. The school is committed to providing quality education and creating an environment where students can develop knowledge, skills, discipline and confidence for a better life.


Our motto is "Education for better life."
